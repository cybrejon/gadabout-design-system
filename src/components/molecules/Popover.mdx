# Popover

Displays rich content in a portal, triggered by a button.

<Canvas of={PopoverStories.Default} />

<Controls />

## Usage Guidelines

Popovers use [Bits UI Popover](https://bits-ui.com/docs/components/popover) under the hood. They are ideal for displaying additional information or small forms without navigating away from the current page.

```html
<script>
  import { Popover, Button } from 'gadabout-design-system';
</script>

<Popover>
  {#snippet trigger()}
    <Button>Open Popover</Button>
  {/snippet}

  <div>
    <h4 class="font-medium">Settings</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-foreground-alt">Configure your preferences here.</p>
  </div>
</Popover>
```

### When to use

- Use for providing additional context or information about a specific element.
- Use for small forms or settings that don't require the full attention of a Modal.
- Use when you want to show content that is contextual to a specific trigger.

### When not to use

- Do not use for large amounts of content or complex workflows; consider a Modal instead.
- Do not use for simple tooltips (short text on hover); use the Tooltip component.

### Best practices

- Ensure the trigger is clearly labeled.
- Keep the content concise and focused.
- Provide a clear way to close the popover (usually by clicking outside or a close button inside).
